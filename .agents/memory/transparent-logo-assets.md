---
name: Transparent logo assets
description: Guidance for verifying and cleaning downloaded logo transparency.
---

Downloaded images labeled “transparent” must be visually checked on the actual
page and inspected for a real alpha channel before use.

**Why:** Image-search results can contain a baked white/gray checkerboard, and
automatic background removal may preserve it rather than creating transparency.

**How to apply:** For future logo assets, verify corner pixels are transparent
and preview the result against the page background before considering it done.
